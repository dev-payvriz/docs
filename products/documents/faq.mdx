## General

### Which documents are accepted?

Tax invoices and utility bills. Delivery challans, rent agreements, bank statements and other documents return `IDTO_202` with `data.document_type` set to what idto detected: `DELIVERY_CHALLAN`, `RENT_AGREEMENT`, `BANK_STATEMENT` or `OTHER`.

### What do I get back for an accepted document?

`document_type` (`TAX_INVOICE` or `UTILITY_BILL`), the `buyer` the bill is addressed to and the `vendor` that issued it (each with `name`, `address`, `gstin`, `pan`, `contact`, `email`, `state` and `state_code`), `invoice_details` (`invoice_number`, `invoice_date` as `YYYY-MM-DD`, `order_reference`), `line_items` (`description`, `hsn_sac`, `quantity`, `unit_rate`, `total_amount`), `totals` (`cgst`, `sgst`, `igst`, `grand_total`, `amount_in_words`) and `bank_details`. Treat every field as optional: a value missing from the document comes back `null`.

### Does idto verify the GSTIN or bank account on the invoice?

No. Invoice OCR returns what is printed. Verify a GSTIN with [GST basic](/api-reference/business/gst-verification-basic) or a bank account with [bank verification](/api-reference/bank/bank-verification).

### Can I use it for address verification?

Yes, that is its main use. For a utility bill or invoice addressed to your user, `buyer.address` is the address on the bill. Compare `buyer.name` with the name you hold before you accept the address.

### What does the no invoice details message mean?

The document was read, but none of the buyer's address, the vendor's name, the invoice number or the grand total could be found on it. You get `IDTO_202` with `data: null` and a message asking for a tax invoice or a utility bill that shows the billing address.

## Billing

### Am I charged when the document is rejected?

Yes. A rejection by type or a document with no invoice details is HTTP 200 with `IDTO_202` and `billed: true`, because the document was read. Errors (non-2xx) are never billed.

### Why did a call inside a workflow come back with billed false?

When you send `workflow_session_token` as a form field for a workflow that already charges for the address step, the individual call is not charged again and `billed` is `false`.

## Technical

### How do I upload the file?

Send `multipart/form-data` with the document in the `file` field. JPEG, PNG and PDF are accepted. Any other type, or an empty file, returns `IDTO_001` (400) and is not billed.

### How large can the file be?

Keep it under 5 MB. The request itself is capped at 12 MB, but a file above 5 MB can be rejected by the reader with `IDTO_503` (413), which is not billed. Compress a large scan or send only the pages that hold the bill.

### What does IDTO_008 mean on this endpoint?

The reader is at capacity or the service is busy. It is HTTP 503 and not billed; retry with backoff.

### Can I retry a timed-out upload safely?

Yes. Send an `Idempotency-Key` header. Replaying the same key with the same file within 48 hours returns the first response without a new charge. Replaying the key with a different file returns `IDTO_010` (422).

### Which errors should I retry?

Retry `IDTO_006` (429) and `IDTO_008` (503) with backoff and the same `Idempotency-Key`. Do not retry `IDTO_001` or `IDTO_503` with the same file; fix or replace it. Contact support on `IDTO_007` (500).

## End users

### How do I tell the user what they uploaded?

On a type rejection, read `data.document_type` (for example `BANK_STATEMENT`) and show the `message`, which names the detected type and asks for a tax invoice or utility bill.

### What should I ask for when the upload fails?

On `IDTO_503`, ask for a smaller or clearer file. On `IDTO_202` with `data: null`, ask for a bill that clearly shows the billing address and invoice details, photographed flat and in full.
