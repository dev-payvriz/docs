## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which documents are accepted?">
    Tax invoices and utility bills. Delivery challans, rent agreements, bank statements and other documents return `IDTO_202` with `data.document_type` set.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Am I charged when the document is rejected?">
    Yes. A rejection by type or a document with no invoice details is HTTP 200 with `IDTO_202` and `billed: true`, because the document was read. Errors (non-2xx) are never billed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does idto verify the GSTIN or bank account on the invoice?">
    No. Invoice OCR returns what is printed. Verify a GSTIN or a bank account with the matching verification endpoint.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Technical

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I upload the file?">
    Send `multipart/form-data` with the document in the `file` field, at most 12 MB.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does IDTO_008 mean on this endpoint?">
    The reader is at capacity or the service is busy. It is HTTP 503 and not billed; retry with backoff.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I retry a timed-out upload safely?">
    Yes. Send an `Idempotency-Key` header. Replaying the same key with the same file within 48 hours returns the first response without a new charge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I tell the user what they uploaded?">
    On a type rejection, read `data.document_type` (for example `BANK_STATEMENT`) and show the `message`, which names the detected type and asks for a tax invoice or utility bill.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
