## General

### Which endpoint should I use?

Use [Verify a driving licence](/api-reference/driving-vehicle/driving-licence) to confirm a licence against the issuing record. Use [Driving licence OCR](/api-reference/driving-vehicle/dl-ocr) to pre-fill the licence number and date of birth from a photo of the card. Use [Vehicle RC](/api-reference/driving-vehicle/vehicle-rc) to look up a vehicle's registration by its number.

### Can I verify a licence from a photo alone?

Read the photo with Driving licence OCR, then send the extracted `data.dl_number` as `licence_number` and `data.dob` as `dob` to Verify a driving licence. OCR on its own only tells you what is printed on the card. Let the user confirm the values you read before you verify them.

### What does licence verification return?

`dl_number`, `name`, `dob`, `relative_name`, `address`, `pincode`, `status`, `issuing_authority`, `validity`, `vehicle_classes`, `blood_group` and `photo`, from the issuing record. Check `status` and `validity` to confirm the licence is current, and `vehicle_classes` to confirm it covers the vehicle your user will drive.

### What does licence OCR return?

What is printed on the card: `dl_number`, `name`, `relative_name`, `dob`, `doi` (date of issue), `doe` (date of expiry), `blood_group`, `address` and `pincode`. Dates come back as `YYYY-MM-DD`, which Verify a driving licence accepts as is.

### Is Vehicle RC billed the same way as the v2 endpoints?

No. Vehicle RC is a v1 endpoint. It is charged only when `status` is `success`, and a charged response carries `chargeble: "true"`. It has no `billed` field and no `error_code` on success.

## Input and validation

### Which date formats does driving licence verification accept?

`YYYY-MM-DD`, `DD-MM-YYYY` and `DD/MM/YYYY`. A missing or unreadable date returns `IDTO_001` (400), "A valid date of birth is required", and is not billed.

### How should I format the licence number?

Send it as printed. Spaces and hyphens are ignored; at least 8 letters and digits must remain. For example `KA0120200000001` and `KA01 20200000001` are both accepted. An empty or too short number returns `IDTO_001` (400) without a lookup.

### What does Vehicle RC need?

`vehicle_number`, plus optional `user_id` and `workflow_session_token`. A body without `vehicle_number` returns 422 and is not charged.

## Billing

### Am I charged when the licence is not found?

Yes. A well-formed request that finds no matching record returns HTTP 200 with `error_code: "IDTO_202"` and `billed: true`, because the check ran to a definitive answer. Errors (non-2xx) are never billed.

### Am I charged when OCR cannot read a licence?

Yes. An image with no readable licence returns HTTP 200 with `IDTO_202`, "No driving licence could be read from the document", and `billed: true`. Ask for a clearer photo.

### Why did a call inside a workflow come back with billed false?

When you pass `workflow_session_token` of an in-progress verification session that charges for that step, the individual call is not charged again. On the v2 endpoints `billed` is `false`; on Vehicle RC the response carries no `chargeble: "true"`.

## Technical

### Can I retry a timed-out call safely?

On the two v2 endpoints, send an `Idempotency-Key` header. Replaying the same key and body within 48 hours returns the first response without a new charge. Vehicle RC has no idempotency support, so a retry can be charged twice.

### What file does licence OCR accept?

JPEG, PNG or PDF in a multipart field named `file`. Keep it under 5 MB: the request is capped at 12 MB, but the reader can reject anything above 5 MB with `IDTO_503` (413). Neither an oversized nor an unsupported file is billed; an unsupported type returns `IDTO_001` (400).

### Which errors should I retry?

Retry `IDTO_006` (429) and `IDTO_008` (503) with backoff and the same `Idempotency-Key`. Do not retry `IDTO_001` without fixing the input. Contact support on `IDTO_007` (500).

### Why are some Vehicle RC fields null?

The registry does not hold every field for every vehicle. Every documented key is always present; a value is `null` when the registry does not hold it.

## End users

### What should I tell a driver whose licence is not found?

Ask them to check the licence number and date of birth against the card and try again. If they confirm both and the result is still `IDTO_202`, send the application to manual review rather than retrying, since each attempt is billed.
