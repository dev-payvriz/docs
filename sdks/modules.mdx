A workflow template is an ordered list of modules. Each module has a slug. You see slugs in `step` on `onStepComplete`, as keys of `accumulated_data`, and you pass them in `pre_verified` and `skippable` (`preVerified` and `skippable` on native SDKs).

Every SDK runs the same flow, so every module below works the same way on Web, Android, iOS, Flutter and React Native. Which modules run, and in what order, is set by the workflow template.

## Identity modules

- `mobile_verification`: Confirms a mobile number with a one-time code.
- `aadhar_verification`: Verifies Aadhaar through DigiLocker or OTP.
- `pan_verification`: Captures or enters a PAN and gets it verified.
- `face_match`: Takes a selfie, checked for liveness and matched against a reference photo.
- `driving_licence`: Verifies a driving licence.
- `vehicle_rc`: Verifies a vehicle registration certificate.
- `account_verification`: Verifies a bank account.
- `address_verification`: Uploads an invoice; the address on it is read and returned.
- `govt_id_selection`: Chooses which government ID to verify; the workflow branches on the choice.
- `name_match`: Runs without a screen at the end of the flow and compares the names collected by other steps.

## Business modules

- `business_type`: Chooses the business type; the workflow branches on the choice.
- `business_pan`: Verifies the business PAN.
- `gst_verification`: Verifies a GST registration.
- `cin_verification`: Verifies a Corporate Identification Number.

## eSign

The `e_sign` slug exists in the registry but only shows a completion screen inside the SDK. To collect a signature, use the eSign API. See [eSign](/products/esign/overview).

## Reading module results

Each finished module adds an entry under its slug in `accumulated_data`, and `onStepComplete` delivers the same result in `result`. Use these to update your UI. Confirm the outcome from your server before you act on it, see [Confirm a session result](/recipes/confirm-session-result).
