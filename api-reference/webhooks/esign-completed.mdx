title: "eSign completed webhook"

---

idto checks open eSign dockets in the background. When every signer of a document has signed, idto archives the signed PDF and then POSTs this event once per document. It carries no PDF bytes: call [Download the signed document](/api-reference/esign/esign-document) with `docket_id` and `document_id` to get it.

- `tranx_id` is the `tranx_id` of the `POST /verify/esign` call that created the docket.
- `event_id` has the form `evt_esign_<tranx_id>_<document_id>_signed`.
- idto watches a docket for 90 days after it was created. A docket not fully signed by then sends no event.

## Headers

| Header | Value |
|---|---|
| `X-Idto-Event-Id` | Same as `event_id` in the body. Stable across retries, so use it to drop duplicates. |
| `X-Idto-Timestamp` | Unix seconds when idto signed the delivery. |
| `X-Idto-Signature` | `sha256=<hex>`: HMAC-SHA256 of `<timestamp>.<raw body>` keyed with your signing secret. |

Verify the signature over the exact bytes you received before parsing the JSON, and reject a timestamp more than 300 seconds old. Code for seven languages: [Verify webhook signatures](/recipes/verify-webhook-signatures). Setup, retries and secret rotation: [Webhooks](/concepts/webhooks).
